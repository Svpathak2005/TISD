TISD

Technology Innovation for Sustainable Development

TISD is a 2-credit course for second-semester Engineering students focusing on innovative solutions for sustainable development through NGO collaboration and entrepreneurship opportunities.

Course Objectives

Innovation

Develop innovative and sustainable solutions for real-world problems

NGO Collaboration

Partner with NGOs to implement solutions in real-world scenarios

Entrepreneurship

Explore startup opportunities and business potential of solutions

Field Visits

Students will participate in organized field trips to companies and organizations working in sustainable development.
